# Verified Moroccan fiscal facts (2026) — content grounding

> Researched 2026-09-18, multi-source. Cite CGI 2026 / note circulaire DGI n°737 as authority on live pages. Figures marked "confirm" need a final check vs the DGI guide before publishing as hard numbers.

## IS — Impôt sur les Sociétés (proportional, NOT progressive) — 2026 target rates
- Bénéfice net < 100 M DH (most SMEs): **20%**
- Bénéfice net ≥ 100 M DH: **35%**
- Financial sector (banks/insurance): **40%**
- Cotisation minimale: 0,25% of CA, floor **3 000 DH**; pay the higher of IS or CM; 4 quarterly acomptes.
- CSS (contribution sociale de solidarité) prorogée 2026–2028 on profits.

## TVA — only TWO rates as of 2026 (reform 2024→2026 abolished 7% & 14%)
- **20%** standard (default: conseil, informatique, électronique, B2B général…)
- **10%** reduced (hébergement/restauration, transport, banque/crédit, énergies renouvelables, eau…)
- 0% exportations (with deduction right); some exemptions (enseignement, actes médicaux, pharma art.92).
- Declaration: monthly if CA > 1M DH, quarterly if ≤ 1M DH; file/pay before the 20th. SIMPL-TVA on tax.gov.ma.
- ⚠️ Do NOT present 7%/14% as current — historical only.

## IR — Impôt sur le Revenu (barème revised by LF 2025, kept 2026)
| Tranche annuelle (DH) | Taux | Somme à déduire (DH) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 40 000 | 0% | 0 |
| 40 001 – 60 000 | 10% | 4 000 |
| 60 001 – 80 000 | 20% | 10 000 |
| 80 001 – 100 000 | 30% | 18 000 |
| 100 001 – 180 000 | 34% | 22 000 |
| > 180 000 | 37% | 27 400 |
- IR = (RNI × taux) − somme à déduire. Déduction charge de famille 600 DH/pers (max 6). Exemption threshold now 40 000 (was 30 000); top 37% (was 38%).

## Auto-entrepreneur
- Ceilings: **500 000 DH** commerce/industrie/artisanat; **200 000 DH** prestations de services. Exceed 2 consecutive years → radiation.
- IR libératoire on CA encaissé: **0,5%** commerce/industrie; **1%** services (confirm vs DGI guide — launched at 1%/2%).
- 80 000 DH/client rule (LF 2023, still active): for services, surplus over 80k with a single client → excluded from régime, 30% retenue à la source libératoire by the client.
- Hors champ TVA, no RC, light bookkeeping, RNAE registration.

## CNSS (payroll 2026)
- Employer ~**21,09%**, employee ~**6,74%** (total ~27,83%).
- Prestations sociales branch capped at **6 000 DH/mois**; AMO uncapped (employer 4,11% / employee 2,26%); allocations familiales 6,40% employer; TFP 1,60% employer.
- Employer registration within 30 days; declarations via Damancom.

## Création SARL / SARL AU (loi 5-96, mod. 24-10)
- **No legal minimum capital** (since loi 24-10); ~10 000 DH recommended for bank credibility; capital blocking required only if capital > 100 000 DH (≥¼ libéré at constitution).
- Steps: (1) certificat négatif OMPIC (~230 DH, 24–48h, valid 3 months) → (2) statuts → (3) blocage capital si >100k → (4) enregistrement des actes (droit ~1% capital, min 1 000 DH) → (5) immatriculation RC (tribunal de commerce / CRI) → (6) ICE + Identifiant Fiscal + TVA + Taxe Professionnelle → (7) affiliation CNSS → (8) publicité légale (BO + journal d'annonces).
- Timeline: **5–10 jours ouvrés** with complete file (CRI centralizes).
- Indicative official/legal cost ≈ **3 000–6 000 DH** excl. fiduciaire fees — present as "à partir de / indicatif".
- Commissaire aux comptes obligatoire only if CA > 50 M DH.

## LF 2026 headline changes (SMEs)
- E-invoicing (facturation électronique) reinforced 2026.
- TVA retenue à la source on service remunerations from **1 Jan 2026**.
- 5% RAS on rental income to legal persons from **1 Jul 2026**.
- +2% enregistrement on cash property/fonds de commerce transfers > 300 000 DH from 1 Jul 2026.
- Family-charge IR deduction raised 500→600 DH/person.

## Target cities (priority order, verified spelling)
El Jadida (HQ, hub) → **Jorf Lasfar** (major industrial-port, OCP/TAQA, strong B2B) → Azemmour (~15km) → Sidi Bennour (chef-lieu province voisine, agro-industrie) → Settat (chef-lieu province, industrie/université) → Oualidia (côtier, tourisme/ostréiculture) → Bir Jdid (périurbain, long-tail) → Casablanca (secondary, high competition → long-tail only).
